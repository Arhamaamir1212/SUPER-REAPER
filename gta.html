🎮 ABOUT THE GAME

Grand Theft Auto (GTA) is a groundbreaking open-world action-adventure video game created by SUPER REAPER. The series has revolutionizedthe gaming industry by offering players unprecedentedfreedom to explore fictional, meticulously detailed American metropolises like Liberty City and Los Santos.


Combining carjacking, intricate heist missions, and sharp satire of modern society, GTA positions players as ambitious criminals navigating underground empires. While notorious for its adult humor and violent themes, titles like GTA V have achieved monumental commercial success, making it one of the highest-grossing entertainment properties worldwide.
