🎲 ABOUT THE GAME

Ludo Strike is a classic strategy board game. Designed for two to four players, the game features a square board divided into four vibrant, colored yards: blue, yellow, green, and red. Each player controls four tokens and aims to race them clockwise around the track.

Movement relies on the roll of a single six-sided die. Players can actively capture opponents' pieces by landing on the same square, forcing them back to the start. Blending luck and clever planning, the first person to safely guide all four tokens to the center home wins.

🏆 GAME FEATURES

🎲

Roll the Dice

Use the six-sided die to determine how far your tokens can move around the board.

⚔️

Capture Opponents

Land on an opponent's square to send their token back to the start and gain an advantage.

🏆

Race to Victory

Guide all four of your tokens safely around the board and reach the center home before your opponents.
