🎮 ABOUT THE GAME

Minecraft is a universally acclaimed sandbox video game that drops players into an infinite, blocky 3D world.

With no set objectives, players enjoy total freedom to explore diverse biomes, gather raw materials, and reshape their environment block by block.

The game features two primary modes: Creative Mode, which provides unlimited resources and limitless building, and Survival Mode, where players must craft tools, manage hunger, and defend against hostile nighttime creatures like zombies and creepers.

⚔ GAME MODES

🏗️

CREATIVE MODE

Build whatever you can imagine with unlimited resources. Fly around the world, create massive structures, and focus entirely on creativity without worrying about survival.

🧟

SURVIVAL MODE

Gather materials, craft tools, manage your resources, explore dangerous areas, and defend yourself from hostile creatures as you fight to survive.
